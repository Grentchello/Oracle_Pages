**Why so bad:**
1. **Hard stop bug** — biggest issue, just fixed in v6.2. ~277 losers × avg -45% × avg $5 actual loss = -1.2 SOL just from this.
2. **Win rate 46%** — under 50%, so any symmetric strategy loses. Need >55% to break even with the current win/loss size asymmetry.
3. **Loss size > win size** — losers ride to -50% (or worse pre-fix), winners sold at +30-50%. Need either tighter losers OR bigger winners.
4. **Position size 0.1 SOL** × 5 concurrent positions = 25% deployed. A -100% loss across all 5 = -25% portfolio in one bad batch.

**Quick wins to try:**
- **Reduce position to 0.05 SOL** (5 positions × 0.05 = 25% deployed, max loss per trade = $5)
- **Tighter stop at -30%** (cut losers 2x faster, accept lower precision for capital preservation)
- **Let winners run to +200%** before partial sell (currently sold at +30% which is too early)
