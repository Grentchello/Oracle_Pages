### RPC bug found and fixed today (2026-11-17)
- `getTokenAccountsByOwner` rejects a filter map containing BOTH `programId` and `mint` (-32602
  'expected map with a single key'). The WSOL helper used exactly that call, so it silently returned
  0.0 for EVERY controller. End cash was understated and the reported cash change was garbage:
  one controller showed -109,368 SOL. Corrected, it holds 4,228,046 SOL of wrapped SOL.
- Fixed measurement: getTokenAccountsByOwner with a MINT-only filter plus an amount dataSlice,
  with every account's owner field verified against the controller address.
- Independent reconciliation: 8/8 sample accounts match after rent of the sampled WSOL accounts
  match exactly via getBalance once the 0.00151384 SOL SPL token-account rent is subtracted
  (including the two largest: 109,361 SOL and 85,096 SOL). The position is real.
- Its shape: 691,851 WSOL accounts holding 4,228,012 SOL - a WSOL dust farm, not a directional bet.
- It holds 4,228,046 SOL in total (~0.7047% of Solana's supply).

## Method note
- Realised PnL computed from native SOL + WSOL balance deltas, reconciled against the controller's own
  starting/ending balance. Cash change is anchored on the oldest fetchable transaction.
- Wallet signature histories are paged to the true end; where the 60,000-signature cap is hit the
  covered time span is measured from block timestamps and reported as a truncated window.
- Holdings are fetched with MINT-FILTERED token queries: a full `getTokenAccountsByOwner` dump
  OOM-kills this box (4 GiB cgroup limit) on wallets with thousands of token accounts.
- GMGN's `native_transfer` funding tag is not confirmable on-chain.
