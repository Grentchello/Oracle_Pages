## Method note
- Token-account layout parsed directly: mint 0..32 | owner 32..64 | amount 64..72.
- A system account is owned by the System Program (11111111111111111111111111111111); an ATA
  is owned by the Token program and holds exactly 0.00151384 SOL of rent. Both were checked.
- Closed token accounts were recovered from the closing transaction's preTokenBalances.
- Cash change is derived from the WINDOW's own transactions (oldest tx's pre-state to newest
  tx's post-state), so it reconciles exactly even though 1 wallet kept trading during
  the sweep. The balance-anchored snapshot is reported separately with its drift.
- Four corrections were needed to make the reconciliation exact:
  1. WSOL deltas must be tracked alongside native deltas - a wrap moves SOL between the
     two channels, so a native-only sum cannot equal a (native + WSOL) cash change.
  2. preBalances/postBalances are indexed by the DEDUPLICATED account key list; summing
     every index that matches the wallet double-counts a duplicated key.
  3. A REVERTED transaction still charges its fee to the signer, so its balance delta is
     not zero. Skipping reverted txs loses exactly their fees; they are counted as
     passive outflows, not funding.
  4. PASSIVE outflows must be published, not hidden: SOL that left in a tx with no token
     trade and no inbound funding (plain transfers, settlement routing to third-party
     wallets). Without that term the decomposition does not add up to the headline.
- Wallets whose signature history is truncated are reported as NOT MEASURABLE, never guessed.
- GMGN's `native_transfer` funding tag is not confirmable on-chain.
