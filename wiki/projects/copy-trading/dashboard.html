This means Solana memecoin "smart money" is fundamentally BURSTY: they hit hard for a day or two, then disappear. Consistent daily-trading wallets simply don't appear in GMGN's token-traders endpoint.
What this means for copy-trading:
  • The bursts are usually very large — 100-1000+ transactions in 1-2 days = sniper campaigns on a token launch
  • Wallets aren't reliably active on a daily basis like equity traders
  • Real copy-trading requires watching for a wallet to come alive, not steady monitoring
  • Alternative: copy token-specific patterns (which tokens they buy) instead of wallets
Workaround: The 50 wallets shown here were ranked by active_days × tx_count × PnL_size — best combination of consistency and profit. The "🤏 Small PnL" filter (under 20 SOL) shows the most realistic targets for a small copy-trading account.
