# oracle_Vault

Grant's personal knowledge wiki. Curated by Grant, compiled and cross-linked by Hermes Agent.

## Structure

- `SCHEMA.md` — conventions, frontmatter, tag taxonomy
- `index.md` — content catalog (find pages here)
- `log.md` — chronological action log
- `raw/` — immutable source material (articles, papers, transcripts, assets)
- `entities/` — notable things (people, orgs, products, models)
- `concepts/` — topics and ideas
- `comparisons/` — side-by-side analyses
- `queries/` — filed answers worth keeping
- `_meta/` — internal notes (topic maps, archived content)

## How it works

Grant points at sources (URLs, files, pastes) or asks questions. Hermes ingests them into
`raw/`, writes or updates wiki pages in the appropriate folder, and links everything
together. Sources never get modified — corrections and synthesis live in the wiki pages.

See `SCHEMA.md` for the full rules.
